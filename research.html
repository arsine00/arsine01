01 ONGOING RESEARCH

Ligand-responsive artificial tRNA systems

Engineering artificial tRNAs that enable ligand-dependent selection of amino acid charging, with a focus on tyrosine and serine.

My current work investigates artificial tRNAs containing aptamer sequences for specific ligands, and explores the potential of these ligand-aptamer systems for translational control mediation ultimately targetting human cells.
